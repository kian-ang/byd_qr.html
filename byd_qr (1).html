03 · ACCIDENT GUIDE

What to Do After an Accident

Follow these five steps after an accident. Always prioritize safety.

01

Prioritize Safety

  • Check that everyone is safe.
  • Call emergency services if anyone is injured.
  • Provide first aid only if you can do so safely.
02

Contact Authorities

  • Contact the police when necessary.
  • Obtain a police report when applicable.
03

Gather Information

  • Record the drivers' license details.
  • Gather vehicle registration information.
  • Collect the other driver's insurance details.
  • Photograph the damage and accident scene.
04

Notify the Insurance Team

  • Report the accident as soon as possible.
  • Provide photographs and available documents.

04 · CLAIM REQUIREMENTS

Prepare Your Documents

Preparing the necessary documents can help the dealership and insurer process your claim.

A. Documents to Prepare

  1. Copy of the driver's license with OR.
  2. Vehicle LTO Certificate of Registration and Official Receipt.
  3. Photographs of the vehicle damage.
  4. Copy of the insurance policy and premium payment receipt.
  5. Police Incident Report or notarized Affidavit of Accident, as applicable.

B. What Happens Next?

  1. The dealership notifies the insurance partner.
  2. The service workshop prepares a repair estimate and photographs.
  3. The dealership submits the required documents to the insurer.
  4. The team follows up on the Letter of Authority (LOA).
  5. The customer is informed if additional documents are needed.
  6. The customer receives updates on the claim and repair progress.
Keep copies of your documents. Additional requirements may apply depending on your policy and the circumstances of the accident.
